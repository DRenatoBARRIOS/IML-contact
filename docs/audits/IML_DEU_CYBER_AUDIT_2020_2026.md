# IML — Germany cyber-resilience audit 2020–2026

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Scope:** Germany (DEU)  
**Method:** Same incident-outcome review applied to France.  
**Result:** Security **88 → 80**, Learning **65 → 70**, overall **81 → 80/100**.

## Method

The review cross-checks formal cybersecurity structures against observed outcomes:

- availability and continuity of care;
- confidentiality or data exfiltration;
- restoration delay;
- victim or customer notification;
- regulatory or judicial outcome where public;
- documented corrective action;
- evidence of learning and follow-through.

Transparency about incidents is not penalised. The assessment concerns demonstrated resilience and correction.

## Score revision

### Security: 88 → 80

The previous score relied strongly on the formal German security framework. The incident review adds direct evidence of material operational disruption and confidentiality exposure. Germany retains strong formal controls and several examples of effective degraded-mode continuity, so the evidence supports a moderate rather than France-sized correction.

### Learning: 65 → 70

Several cases document a visible sequence of detection, investigation, notification, mitigation, regulatory action or technical correction. The score is not raised further because national complaint-to-resolution statistics, compensation outcomes and final judicial closure remain incomplete.

## Limitation

This is a national evidence profile, not a statement about every German hospital. Public incident documentation remains uneven, and absence of a published judicial or regulatory outcome does not prove absence of action.
